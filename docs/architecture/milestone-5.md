# Milestone 5 — Management Intelligence

Milestone 5 adds initiative-centered management intelligence to the single application-owned runtime.

## Implemented boundary

- Tenant/user-scoped initiatives with eight categorical health dimensions and no overall score.
- Outcomes with explicit `direct`, `contributing`, `correlated`, or `unknown` attribution.
- Commitments with reason-required date changes and preserved drift history.
- Evidence-backed positive, risk, outcome-gap, execution-risk, and coaching-opportunity signals.
- A high-significance Attention inbox with confirm, context, disagree, dismiss, and outdated corrections.
- PM evidence profiles, 1:1 preparation, PM review, weekly leadership review, portfolio review, and decision debt.
- Structured plus rendered management artifacts on traced, deterministic workflows.
- A 60-case management evaluation catalog with false management signal rate as the critical metric.

## Epistemic and fairness boundary

Activity is never treated as product progress or PM performance. Missing records describe a documentation gap, not absence of thinking or work. Outcome timing does not establish causality. Coaching patterns require three distinct initiatives and remain manager-reviewable interpretations. No score, rank, personality inference, or automated personnel decision exists.

## Known limitations

- Signals use only records currently stored in ProductOS; disconnected or inaccessible systems remain unknown.
- Product and customer quality concerns are not inferred from ticket activity.
- Owner IDs are identifiers, not a full identity or org-directory integration.
- The deterministic rules are intentionally conservative and need production data evaluation before wider use.
- Milestone 6 proactivity, scheduling, notifications, and external delivery are not included.
