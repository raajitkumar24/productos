# ADR 0011: No employee scoring or ranking

Status: Accepted

## Context

A composite employee score is opaque, easily confounds activity with outcomes, hides uncertainty, and can turn incomplete internal data into an unjustified personnel judgment.

## Decision

ProductOS will not calculate, persist, expose, or visualize employee scores, ranks, personality assessments, or automated performance ratings. PM profiles contain separately inspectable responsibilities, observations, outcomes with attribution labels, strengths, coaching questions, risks, and evidence limitations. Management signals are correctable and never represent an employment decision.

## Consequences

Leaders must exercise judgment using the underlying evidence and context. ProductOS can organize that evidence and challenge assumptions, but it cannot replace accountable human management or formal performance processes.
